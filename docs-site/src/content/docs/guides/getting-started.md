This guide takes you from nothing to a connected wallet on [vezo.exchange](https://www.vezo.exchange). Nothing here requires prior Mezo experience.

## 1. Choose a wallet

Vezo supports two wallet families:

- **EVM wallets**: MetaMask, Rabby, and anything WalletConnect-compatible.
- **Bitcoin wallets**: Unisat, OKX Wallet, and Xverse, connected through Mezo Passport.

If you're unsure, MetaMask is the simplest starting point.

## 2. Add the Mezo network

The Vezo app will prompt you to add and switch networks automatically when you connect. To add it manually in MetaMask (*Settings → Networks → Add network*):

**Mainnet**

```
Network Name:   Mezo
RPC URL:        https://rpc.mezo.org
Chain ID:       31612
Currency:       BTC
Explorer:       https://explorer.mezo.org
```

**Testnet** (for experimenting with free funds)

```
Network Name:   Mezo Testnet
RPC URL:        https://rpc.test.mezo.org
Chain ID:       31611
Currency:       BTC
Explorer:       https://explorer.test.mezo.org
```

:::tip[Practice on testnet first]
Vezo runs fully on both networks, and the app has a network switcher in the header. Grab free testnet BTC from [faucet.test.mezo.org](https://faucet.test.mezo.org) and do a practice listing or purchase end-to-end before touching real funds.
:::

## 3. Fund your wallet

You'll need:

- **BTC on Mezo** for gas on every transaction (amounts are tiny), and as a payment currency.
- **MEZO or MUSD** only if you plan to buy listings priced in those tokens. Where a swap route exists you can also [pay with a different token](/concepts/pay-with-any-token/).

On mainnet, bridge assets to Mezo via the official Mezo bridge at [mezo.org](https://mezo.org).

## 4. Connect to Vezo

1. Open [vezo.exchange](https://www.vezo.exchange).
2. Click **Connect** in the header and pick your wallet.
3. Confirm the network. The Mainnet/Testnet switcher is next to the connect button.

That's it. There's no sign-up and no deposit; your wallet is your account.
