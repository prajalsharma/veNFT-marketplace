**Vezo** is the first secondary marketplace for veNFTs on [Mezo](/introduction/what-is-mezo/): an escrowless, peer-to-peer market for veBTC and veMEZO vote-escrowed positions. Sellers keep custody of their NFT until the moment of sale, and every trade settles atomically in a single transaction.

If you're new, start with [What is Vezo?](/introduction/what-is-vezo/) To use the marketplace right away, see [Getting Started](/guides/getting-started/). The app itself is at [vezo.exchange](https://www.vezo.exchange).
